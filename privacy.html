Privacy policy

Hermes is a private assistant used only by members of the Aquino family, each on their own computer.

When a family member signs in with Google, Hermes uses their Gmail, Calendar and Drive data only to do what that person asks (for example reading, drafting or sending their own email, or managing their own calendar). The sign-in token is stored only on that person's own device.

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: aquinomaximilian@gmail.com
